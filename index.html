Headroom

know your headroom

A personal psychological load management tool built on the stress-bucket model. See your current state. Understand your capacity. Make better decisions about the day ahead.

Learn more
The model

Your mind has a capacity. Life fills it up.

The stress-bucket model is used in CBT and addiction recovery. Stressors fill the bucket. Relief actions drain it. When the bucket overflows, automatic behaviours take over. Headroom makes that process visible.

◧

Load honestly

Each morning, acknowledge every persistent stressor. Legal proceedings. Financial pressure. Relationship strain. The level goes into the red. That is honesty, not failure.

↓

Work it down

Through the day, log relief actions as they happen. Exercise. Connection. Rest. Problem-solving. Watch the level come back toward calm.

◉

See the pattern

Continuous history makes gradual buildups visible over days and weeks. Spot the slow drift before it reaches the point where decisions are no longer yours to make.

Origin

Built from lived experience

Headroom started with a conversation between a service user and a keyworker at Change Grow Live Nottingham. The keyworker introduced the stress-bucket model. The service user, who is a data analyst and developer, built a working application from it.

The bucket model made sense immediately. But I needed to see it. I needed the number in front of me, updating in real time, honest about where I was.

The developer has used Headroom daily since early 2026 as a personal recovery support tool. This is not a product designed in a lab. It was built by someone who needed it and uses it every day.
